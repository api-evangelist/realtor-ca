Everyone’s been put to the test in their career. On this episode of REAL TIME, we invite two members of the REALTOR® community, Anu Joshi-Mehendale and Dusko Sremac, to reflect on their top lessons learned in real estate as they approach a decade of experience in their respective practices. Get advice to help you minimize mistakes, set yourself up for success, and stay motivated in your own practice – new or established.
