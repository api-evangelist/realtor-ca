The beautiful thing about real estate is there are many avenues to success and even more ways to approach it. Bringing over skills from other industries can be a major benefit for people making a career transition into becoming a REALTOR®. Just ask Ryan Elliott and Katharina Lenz, two REALTORS® who have experienced the professional and personal benefits of making the switch from previous careers into real estate.
