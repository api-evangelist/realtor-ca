So, you’re starting to build a name for yourself as a REALTOR ® , but how do you propel your already solid foundation to new heights of success? Continuing our Working REALTOR® series, this episode shares tips, strategies, and lessons learned to help you continue building a successful real estate career, at any phase. Hear from three REALTORS ® from across the country: Ruth Alexander from Calgary, Alberta, Nene Akintan from Oakville, Ontario, and Steve Saretsky from Vancouver, British Columbia.
