It’s a phrase you may have heard hundreds of times, repeated like a mantra: “location, location, location.” REALTORS® know how much hinges on where a property is situated. It’s part of why their role in the home buying and selling journey is so valuable. They also know how to identify potential, compromise needs vs wants, and stay on budget.
