Everyone’s path to truth and reconciliation can look different, but the first step often begins the same way: listening to the stories and experiences of others. Stories can challenge perspectives, open doors for meaningful dialogue, and carry important teachings forward. They can also connect us through shared emotions and experiences, encourage reflection, and help us consider our own role in truth and reconciliation.
